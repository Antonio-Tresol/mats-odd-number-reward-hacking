## Three things about running these sweeps

**Concurrency is the throughput knob, and the curve is not monotone.** At
`--workers 3` the sweep managed 1.2 successes a minute with 40% of calls lost to
upstream 429s. At 10 it managed 8 a minute with almost none. At 24 it produced
eight rows in 75 seconds, all errors. Use 8 to 10.

**A cell that errors mid-run stays short until the next invocation.** `done` is
computed once at startup, so a row that exhausts its retries inside a run is not
resubmitted in that run. A cell will sit at 28 or 29 of 30 indefinitely while
the sweep works on later branch points. Re-invoking the same command fixes it;
any watcher that waits for exactly 30 will hang.

**The grids differ.** `--points 10` on a 205-sentence trace gives 0, 23, 46, 68,
91, …; `--points 20` gives 0, 11, 22, 32, 43, 54, …. They share only the ends
and a few points. Resume keys on `(sentences_kept, index)`, so running both
against one file is safe and simply buys the union.

`--prompt-from` takes its own seed key and its own output file, so a cross-prompt
sweep can neither repeat nor overwrite the same-prompt sweep's draws.
