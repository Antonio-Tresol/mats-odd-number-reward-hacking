Method and caveats

How this was read

Corpus. Every collected rollout after de-duplication (2,921), exported as 108 Markdown chunks of one results file and one treatment each (≤200k characters), with the graded answer from the same two paths grading uses — literal integer, or the judge's cached verdict.

Readers. One agent per chunk, an open-ended prompt: the environment, a legend for the treatments, guiding questions marked "not categories", a rule to quote verbatim with the trace index, and an instruction to check our graded answer against the response. 42 chunks (qwen3.5, qwen3.6, most of qwen3.8) were read by claude-opus-5 before the run was moved to claude-sonnet-5 for cost; 66 by Sonnet. The reader is recorded per chunk.

Syntheses. One claude-opus-5 agent per model over all of its readings, told to reopen traces for any claim, re-verify quotes, and report reader disagreements rather than smooth them. Four Kimi reader quotes failed verbatim checks and were corrected; one qwen3.8 reader quote is not in its file; several readers' prose contradicted their own labels. The syntheses say so.

Caveats. Within-model comparisons were made by one reader model; cross-model comparisons mix Opus-read and Sonnet-read chunks. Readers used their own label vocabularies (1,175 distinct labels), so label counts are never pooled across chunks here — only mechanical recomputes are compared across files. Reader labels in the browser are for finding traces, not for counting them. The readers are an unvalidated instrument; every number on this page is tagged with where it came from.

Grading. Every synthesis re-checked graded answer against response text and found zero errors across ~2,900 traces. The 68 disagreements readers filed are chain-of-thought-versus-response drift (parity survived in every case) or two files that had never been judged and now are.
